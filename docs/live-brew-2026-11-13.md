# Braumeister 20 L Plus live brew, November 13–14, 2026

One brewer compared the MySpeidel portal with Home Assistant during a full brew using a 20 L Plus. Times below are local (CET). The screenshots span several successive card builds. They establish what the tester saw on each screen. The final layouts were also checked against simulated device states. The new card buttons are localized previews. The observed key presses described here were made through MySpeidel.

## Versions and installation

The coordinated beta uses upstream integration commit `c524fe04` (after upstream release 3.2.0). Integration 3.2.1-beta.1 and Bridge 0.5.1-beta.1 retain upstream recipe upload, connection monitoring, HTTP backoff, reauthentication and scan interval fixes. The fork restores the display clock, pump and heater flags, and target and elapsed rest times. Companion v0.1.0-beta.10 provides the read-only recipe picker. Card v0.7.0-beta.12 packages the matching Companion and eight bundled languages.

On this installation the local Bridge failed to start when binding the DNS socket. With AdGuard handling the device DNS rewrite, setting the Bridge option `dns_port: 0` restored the HTTP listener and loopback API. Recipe and temperature messages then arrived and the card came online. The warning entity mapping was already correct. MySpeidel being reachable alone did not establish local Bridge health. During heat-up the device mode could still read “Waiting” while the current step and actuator indicators showed heating.

## Display changes and observed screens

| Card beta | Stage | Observed behavior and card change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| .13 | Late brew prompts | Added localized, disabled previews for malt pipe removal, boil start, hop additions and whirlpool. Later releases refined their labels and positions. |
| .14 | Mash-in | Mash-in temperature reached and add-malt screens show Cancel and Select. The malt confirmation screen also shows Down and Up in the middle. |
| .15–.16 | Mash rests | Heating toward a rest shows Cancel alone. Once the hold timer runs, Select appears at right. The tester confirmed the transition again on the second rest. |
| .17 | Rest end through boil heat-up | Rest end, malt pipe removal, start boil and heating to boil each show Cancel and Select. The lid alarm appeared in HA and MySpeidel at 23:48. HA showed the prompt and both keys with its alarm and pause-related hints. |
| .18 | Hop addition | Cancel and Select flank the prompt. HA identified the first and second additions, and the portal showed their positions out of three. |
| .19 | Timed boil | The four-key preview matches the portal when the target is framed. The tester saw the four keys in HA. The portal can also show only Cancel and Select with the target unframed. |
| .20 | End of brew | Portal reference: four keys at brew finished, then a single right-aligned Select at Whirlpool and Cheers. HA reported all three stages and returned to its four-key main menu. Beta.20 matched the preview layouts in simulated tests. |

Recipe picker retest at 00:07: the tester confirmed all nine names on the HA recipe selection screen, five down the left column and four down the right. Only the selected first recipe was highlighted. The one-recipe symptom is resolved in this installation. Its cause and the particular change responsible were not established.

The recipe page reported six minutes at 23:54, consistent with the remaining time in a ten-minute boil started around 23:50. This is evidence for that step. The generic time label and its footnote still apply elsewhere. During the boil, the portal also showed target/elapsed time and time until the next addition.

## Protocol findings and open questions

- During a running mash rest, Select revealed Down and Up. The portal sent key 3 for Up and key 2 for Down. Both HTTP responses were 200. After Up the arrows disappeared. What value, if any, changes during a rest? Does Down also close the view?
- During the boil, the tester changed the framed temperature target from 98 to 95 °C with the arrow keys. Identify the device status marker for the framed/unframed target and the four/two-key layouts. Verify the increment, when edits take effect, and what closes the editor. See the [timestamped observations](program-screen-controls.md#open-investigation--select-during-a-timed-rest-2026-11-13) for correlation with status frames.
- Determine how to expose total scheduled hop additions, next-addition time and boil target/elapsed time to the card. Verify remaining-time semantics for other stages before changing the general label.
- Establish how the lid alarm can be distinguished from an actual program pause in card hints. Verify device transitions and acknowledgments separately before enabling late-stage controls, including final confirmation and Whirlpool. A successful portal HTTP response alone does not confirm device execution.
- If the one-recipe symptom returns, compare the Companion recipe picker response while selection is open with the Bridge recipe count and list for the same device.

## Verification

At the coordinated release, 316 integration/Bridge tests with 280 subtests and 34 Companion tests passed. Card checks passed through beta.20, including localized previews, interrupt handling and main-menu return. The live comparisons supplement those tests. End-of-brew screenshots were the references used to implement beta.20, rather than a second hardware run of that build.
